# Pull exposure01 - evidence and inferential limits

6 November 2026. Source bibliography, not a parallel E0 authority. EV-71 governs derived roles; EV-70 forbids EMG-to-hypertrophy inference. EV-08/09/10 explain the existing accounting convention. No source below validates a personal exposure percentage. Access levels remain explicit.

## Four distinct propositions

A: anatomical capacity. B: recruitment observed in the studied task. C: plausible hypertrophy-relevant tension. D: ordinal AUREN role under EV-71. A/B/C do not automatically prove D. SECONDARY is a qualitative loaded synergist label, not a measured fraction; MINOR is stabilization. The proposed neutral-only inference must be reviewed as a claim about loaded function, not accepted because a schema wants a value.

## Primary/secondary source checks

### ACKLAND2008 - PRIMARY_FUNCTIONAL_ANATOMY_CADAVER

[Moment arms of the muscles crossing the anatomical shoulder](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1469-7580.2008.00965.x). PMID: 18691376; DOI: 10.1111/j.1469-7580.2008.00965.x.

Access: Publisher full methods/results/table1/discussion inspected; PMC XML inaccessible. Population: 8 entire upper-extremity cadavers

Execution: Tendon-excursion shoulder moment arms, coronal abduction and sagittal flexion, elevation to120degrees.

Result: Middle/inferior PM have adductor leverage; inferior PM is a weak extensor in flexion while clavicular/middle regions differ. Posterior deltoid has large extensor leverage, but adduction leverage changes with angle/plane.

Limits: Capacity, not actual recruitment/force-sharing in a living loaded pulldown; PM/LD lines-of-action based on Visual Human anatomy; no axial-rotation test; no hypertrophy. Avoid saying PD can never adduct.

AUREN use: Anatomical capacity and bounded joint-action inference; reused prior source, not new categorical evidence alone.

### HIK2019 - SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW_ANATOMICAL_MOMENT_ARMS

[The moment arms of the muscles spanning the glenohumeral joint: a systematic review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6284439/). PMID: 30411350; DOI: 10.1111/joa.12903.

Access: PMC abstract and full page available; supporting anatomy context, not independently new primary experiment. Population: Review of geometric and tendon-excursion studies

Execution: Shoulder abduction, flexion, axial rotation, subregional analysis.

Result: PM/LD/teres major substantial depressor/adductor capacity; posterior-deltoid function depends on plane and position.

Limits: Not a primary intervention and not a hypertrophy or actual exercise force-sharing study. Online2018; issue2019Jan.

AUREN use: Cross-check primary anatomical interpretation; do not count as an independent primary replication.

### UW_PECTORALIS - AUTHORITATIVE_ANATOMY_REFERENCE

[Pectoralis Major, University of Washington Radiology Muscle Atlas](https://rad.uw.edu/muscle-atlas/pectoralis-major). PMID: not indexed; DOI: not applicable.

Access: Full anatomy page and official search extract Population: General human functional anatomy, not sample-based exercise trial

Execution: Adduction and medial rotation; sternocostal head extends humerus, clavicular head flexes.

Result: Corroborates PM adductor and sternocostal extension function. NCBI Bookshelf NBK525991 explicitly states extension of flexed arm.

Limits: No load sharing or hypertrophy magnitude; not primary experiment.

AUREN use: Direct authoritative anatomical support for the flexed-humerus extension claim.

### SPERANDEI2009 - PRIMARY_ACUTE_SURFACE_EMG

[Electromyographic analysis of three different types of lat pull-down](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19855327/). PMID: 19855327; DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0b013e3181b8d30a.

Access: ABSTRACT_ONLY; official Ovid full text returned402 payment-required. Population: 24 trained adult men

Execution: 5repetitions at80%1RM; front-of-neck, behind-neck, V-bar bent-elbow pulldowns.

Result: Concentric PM front>V-bar>behind; eccentric PM front/V-bar>behind. LD no difference. Concentric PD behind>front>V-bar; eccentric PD behind>V-bar. Biceps behind>V-bar>front both phases.

Limits: EMG normalized to highest observed technique, not MVIC; geometry/electrode-site details unverified without full text; no growth, force, injury outcomes. Front/behind PD contrast conflicts with Signorile, so no universal rank.

AUREN use: Recruitment corroboration and execution-dependence; cannot alone resolve SECONDARY/MINOR or justify injury claim.

### YOUDAS2010 - PRIMARY_ACUTE_SURFACE_EMG

[Surface electromyographic activation patterns and elbow joint motion during a pull-up, chin-up, or Perfect-Pullup rotational exercise](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21068680/). PMID: 21068680; DOI: 10.1519/JSC.0b013e3181f1598c.

Access: ABSTRACT_ONLY via official EuropePMC core metadata; full journal text not accessed. Population: 21men and4women, young healthy volunteers

Execution: Conventional pronated pull-up, supinated chin-up, rotating handle variation; unassisted bilateral.

Result: PM recruited across variants and more active in chin-up than pull-up; LD also recruited. Authors describe early lower-trapezius/PM and later biceps/LD sequence.

Limits: No assisted or unilateral condition; posterior deltoid not measured; no chronic adaptation. Normalization itself can exceed100%MVIC, showing percentages are not muscle-work shares.

AUREN use: Unassisted pull-up PM recruitment; related-context corroboration only for assisted candidates, not automatic transfer.

### RABELLO2024 - SYSTEMATIC_REVIEW_ACUTE_EMG

[Activation of the three deltoid muscle portions during common strengthening exercises: A systematic review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1360859222001607). PMID: 39593452; DOI: 10.1016/j.jbmt.2022.12.002.

Access: Official abstract plus publisher indexed methods/results; whole review tables not accessed. Population: 33studies; indexed publisher methods reports616participants; do not use pooled demographic details for candidate inference.

Execution: Isotonic exercises at minimum60%1RM or bodyweight; normalized EMG; similar exercises grouped, averaged, ranked.

Result: Pull-up group ranked high for posterior deltoid; standard pull-up and suspended inverted row highest individual variants.

Limits: Heterogeneous normalized acute EMG rankings, not force or hypertrophy roles. Does not establish assisted/unilateral transfer. DOI year2022 and volume40Oct2024 coexist legitimately.

AUREN use: Locator of primary posterior-delt studies only; not categorical role authority.

### BUONSENSO2025 - PRIMARY_ACUTE_SURFACE_EMG

[Electromyographic Analysis of Back Muscle Activation During Lat Pulldown Exercise: Effects of Grip Variations and Forearm Orientation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12452428/). PMID: 40981044; DOI: 10.3390/jfmk10030345.

Access: FULL_TEXT_RELEVANT_METHODS_RESULTS_DISCUSSION via EuropePMC XML. Population: 40men18–35years,≥5years resistance-training experience

Execution: 5reps70%1RM, randomized7variants,2s concentric/2s eccentric; wide1.5biacromial, narrow1.0, pronated/supinated/neutral, upright/30degreeincline.

Result: Full-movement PD omnibusp=.002; wide-pronated30degree>upright wide-pronatedp=.011 and wide-neutralp=.017. LD no detected difference across variants. Pec not recorded.

Limits: Acute male trained sample, no force/hypertrophy; no equivalence design; not factorial for all possible grip/inclination combinations. Phase-specific interpretation should stay within reported analysis. Some discussion generalizations exceed study design; use actual results.

AUREN use: PD participation and execution-specificity, no grip-superiority growth claim; neutral-width not intrinsically fixed by this study.

### URBANCZYK2020 - PRIMARY_BIOMECHANICAL_MODEL_WITH_HUMAN_KINEMATICS

[Avoiding high-risk rotator cuff loading: Muscle force during three pull-up techniques](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/sms.13780). PMID: 32715526; DOI: 10.1111/sms.13780.

Access: Publisher full methods/results/discussion/limitations inspected. Population: 11healthy trained men,>3years pull-up experience

Execution: Unassisted bilateral front-pronated shoulder-width, wide-pronated, reverse-supinated; concentric upward phase.

Result: Variant/phase-specific muscle-force predictions; reverse shows greater early PM peak, posterior-delt described among arm depressors at start. Wide redistributes stress toward back musculature.

Limits: Optimization minimizes squaredstress; no upper muscle-force bound, assumed equal left/right hand forces perpendicular to bar, idealized model; no assistance/unilateral, no direct measured individual-muscle forces or longitudinal hypertrophy/injury incidence.

AUREN use: Mechanistic corroboration that torso path/plane matters and pull-up differs from pulldown; never convert modeledstress to exposurepercent.

### SNARR2017 - PRIMARY_ACUTE_SURFACE_EMG

[Electromyographical Comparison of a Traditional, Suspension Device, and Towel Pull-Up](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5548150/). PMID: 28828073; DOI: 10.1515/hukin-2017-0068.

Access: Full primary methods/results inspected via EuropePMC XML. Population: 15resistance-trained men/women

Execution: 3reps each, traditional/suspension/towel unassisted bilateral pull-ups,1.5biacromialwidth.

Result: Posterior deltoid excitation in all three; no detected PD differences.

Limits: Large dispersion, small sample, acute normalization; authors infer stimulus using EMG thresholds but this inference must NOT be imported to AUREN. No pec measurement.

### SIGNORILE2002 - PRIMARY_ACUTE_SURFACE_EMG

## Relevant longitudinal adaptation

No applicable isolated pectoralis/posterior-deltoid hypertrophy intervention for one of the five exact candidates was identified in the targeted fresh search. This is not an exhaustive absence claim or a registered systematic review. PMID39716392 has pulldown training but not the required muscle-specific imaging endpoint; multi-exercise studies cannot isolate pull-attributed pectoralis growth. ## Two narrative-source mapping hazards

The 2026 narrative is a locator, not the authority for candidate-specific inferences. Its Swann/Barry result concerns bent-over barbell rowing in the primary, not the canonical pulldown. Teixeira PMID35992501 uses straight-arm standing shoulder extension and maximal isometrics, not the seated bent-elbow canonical candidate. Neither can resolve this candidate merely because the review or paper uses the word pulldown.

## Path A versus B: explicit decision package

Inferior/lower-costal PM can contribute an extensor moment to the flexed humerus in the same direction as loaded return fromoverhead, rather than merely hold trunk/scapula fixed. PM excitation occurs in related bent-elbow V-bar/FNL primary protocols. UnderEV71 synergist ratherthan purely stabilizer definition, SECONDARY is a defensible bounded joint_action_anatomy inference; it is not anEMGthreshold assignment. MINOR would require positive stabilization-only argument that current evidence does notprovide.

If source-to-canonicalexecution gap or regionalagonism→zone-mapping remains materially unresolved afterreview, participation-present/category-unresolved accurately preserves it. Do not chooseB solelybecause no trialor no forcepercentage; that addsstricter standard thanEV71.

Strongest counterarguments:

- The same Ackland anatomical capacity was already documented in curation1.0 and judged insufficient. Reusing it without explaining the additional execution-specific reasoning would merely relabel prior uncertainty. New acute corroboration does not independently observe contract-level function.
- Ackland tests elevation only to120degrees and represents subregions. Inferior PM has extensor leverage, while clavicular and middle PM differ; assigning the aggregated pecs zone SECONDARY requires a declared interpretation that a contributing subregion makes the zone a loaded synergist.
- The closest bent-elbow V-bar result is abstract-only. Exact handle width, bar trajectory, torso angle, range, electrode region, and cable geometry cannot be asserted identical to the canonical G17 realization.
- Positive extensor moment capacity plus surface EMG does not directly measure positive agonist mechanical work. Co-contraction/stabilization is possible. However, demanding direct force measurement from every synergist would exceed the present anatomy-based doctrine; the reviewer must judge whether the functional inference is sufficiently specific here.
- Pectoralis is not the principal force generator of the canonical lat-primary task; secondary is an inference from loaded extension, not a claim of known meaningful chest hypertrophy dose or comparable growth to direct chest exercise.
- The specific inference must be invalidated if current execution substitutes a substantially different plane, path, range, attachment, or loading configuration. Neither the name neutral nor a vague front pull licenses all possible executions.
